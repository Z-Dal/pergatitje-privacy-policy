Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pergatitje Automation ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when used to publish content to and moderate comments on the Pergatitje Facebook Page.

What the app does

The app is an internal automation tool used by the Pergatitje Page administrator. It helps publish videos to the Pergatitje Facebook Page, check Page publishing status, and moderate comments on Page posts when enabled by the Page administrator.

Information we collect or access

Depending on the enabled automation, the app may access:

  • Facebook Page ID, Page name, Page access token, post IDs, video IDs, Reel IDs, and publishing status.
  • Content uploaded or managed by the Page administrator, including video files, captions, titles, descriptions, and timestamps.
  • Facebook Page comments and replies, including comment text, commenter name or profile ID when available from Meta, comment ID, post ID, and creation time.
  • Workflow logs needed to confirm whether an upload or moderation action succeeded or failed.

How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Publish administrator-selected videos to the Pergatitje Facebook Page.
  • Track which videos have already been uploaded so the same item is not posted repeatedly.
  • Moderate comments on the Pergatitje Facebook Page according to the Page administrator's rules.
  • Diagnose upload, publishing, scheduling, and moderation errors.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the automation workflow.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with advertisers or data brokers. Information is sent only to the services required to operate the workflow, including Meta/Facebook and the administrator's private n8n automation environment.

Data retention

Workflow records, upload status, post IDs, video IDs, comment IDs, and error logs are kept only as long as needed to operate, audit, and troubleshoot the automation. Temporary downloaded video files are deleted after successful upload. If an upload fails, temporary files may be retained briefly for troubleshooting and then removed by the administrator.

Data deletion requests

If you commented on the Pergatitje Facebook Page and want information associated with your comment removed from our automation logs, contact us at dalianzogaj@gmail.com. Please include the Facebook Page post or comment link if available so we can locate the relevant record. We will review and delete applicable stored workflow data unless we need to retain it for security, legal, or abuse-prevention reasons.

Security

Access tokens and workflow data are stored in the administrator's private automation environment. Access is limited to authorized administrators. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the data used by the automation.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and is used only by the Pergatitje Page administrator for Page publishing and moderation.
